# CREATIVE: the devices only THE MARK could have (2026-09-25)

The founder's bar: the level of thought in chit.tools, not its look. Every device here comes from this product's own vocabulary. If a device would work on any other site, it is not on this list.

## The vocabulary
- **The mark.** A con's victim. Also a line on an instrument, and an ink mark: a stamp.
- **The ticket.** An order is a paper ticket. Paper can be printed, stamped, and torn.
- **The line.** Your limit. Over it, blocked. Under it, clears. Everything on the page sits over or under a line.
- **The tear.** A blocked order is cut down to the size that fits. A ticket stub tears along the perforation.
- **The tape.** Live readings arrive on a strip of paper, like a ticker.
- **The toll.** What the pools keep.

## Devices, and why each one
1. **The cut.** When a blocked order is cut to the size that fits ("Use $X instead"), the ticket tears in two along a jagged line and the pieces fall away, revealing the smaller ticket already stamped CLEARS. The old figures ride on the torn pieces; the new ones are underneath. This is the product in one motion: over the line, cut, cleared. It plays in the app on the cut buttons only (never on a slider drag), and in the story's hero on a loop: print, BLOCKED, tear, CLEARS.
2. **Watch the line.** The pools refill by the hour (our own reading). A blocked ticket can be left to watch: it re-quotes every 30 seconds, draws each reading against the line, and when the cost drops under the line it stamps CLEARS and, if allowed, sends a browser notification. No server, no storage: the ticket waits on the reader's screen. A guard that also waits for you is a genuine feature, not a decoration.
3. **The tape.** A strip of paper across the top of the story carries the live census as a ticker: every stock, its cost on $25,000, moving. The page is alive before anyone scrolls.
4. **Stubs for a rail.** The scene rail on the right is a column of torn ticket stubs, numbered 01 to 09. The current one is pulled out and inked red.
5. **A hanging tag for Launch app.** The call to action is a paper tag with a punched hole, hung from the top bar. It swings on hover.
6. **"mark." is stamped, not typeset.** In the headline, the red word lands with the stamp's slam, once, when the hero enters.
7. **The line through the field.** Scene 4's bars are drawn against a vertical line at 1%. Bars that cross it are red. The line is the same object as on every ticket.
8. **A ticket you can hand over.** The app's stock, amount and limit live in the URL, so a ticket is a link. Paste it and the same ticket prints for someone else.

## Not doing, and why
- A custom cursor shaped like a stamp: a gimmick that slows the pointer.
- A torn divider between two halves of the hero: that is chit's device.
- Sound: nobody wants a stamp sound in a trading tool.
- WebGL paper: a real model would be better than CSS paper, but not in the hours left. Recorded for after the hackathon.

## Rules that stay
Movement and clip only; text never starts invisible; every figure live or dated; reduced motion shows the final state; the Ruler must be clean on every page at 390 and 1440.
