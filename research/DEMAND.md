## 3. Failures in Tokenized Equity (Last 5 Years)

- **Mirror Protocol (Terra, Oct 2021, May 2022):** $90M + $2M exploits; oracle price manipulation caused sAMC/mAssets drainage. Lesson: Pricing mismatches can hollow the protocol.
- **Synthetix sUSD (2025):** Collateral cut from 500% to 200%; lost arbitrage incentive; sUSD depegged and frozen. Lesson: Capital efficiency kills backing if incentives aren't aligned.
- **FTX tokenized stocks (Nov 2022):** Custody fraud: CM Equity ended relationship Dec 2021 but FTX claimed held assets through 2022. No documented ownership of 36 listed stocks. Lesson: Custody opacity kills trust on launch.
- **Binance stock tokens:** Delisted due to regulatory/compliance risk, low liquidity, thin market cap (FXStreet, Cryptonomist Aug 2026).

## 5. Historical Failure Patterns Applied

LEDGE postmortem §1: "Nobody asked for it." This is the kill test. Here: 801K+ holders, $4.9B volume, named complaints (liquidity on small caps, regulatory risk) = demand signals exist and are named. Judges see: real volume, real users, real pain, and regulatory tailwind (SEC approval Sept 17).

But: The after-hours trade volume (63%) and SpaceX IPO spike (99% Solana share) point to a **specific wedge with pull:** traders who need 24/7 access + market-wide liquidity. Infrastructure (custody, price discovery, clearing) and consumer onboarding (Kalshi model) are unproven.

## Closing: What a Judge Would Call Real User & Problem

**Real user:** A trader who holds tokenized stocks and wants to trade them outside Wall Street hours without slippage or regulatory risk. Named by volume: 801K+ addresses, 63% after-hours activity, SpaceX IPO-driven spike.

**Real problem:** (1) Liquidity is thin on non-blue-chip tokenized stocks; (2) regulatory uncertainty consumed six months (resolved Sept 17); (3) custody models vary (EU vs. US); (4) price discovery is fragmented across DEXs. Any app that addresses one of these for a specific wedge (e.g., "24/7 liquidity provisioning for smaller equities") would have pull.

**Why Solana:** 95% of tokenized equity volume. If you own the infra, you own the category. But Robinhood Chain is competing (late July 2026), so time-to-feature matters.
