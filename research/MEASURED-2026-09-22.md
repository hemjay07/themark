`https://hermes.pyth.network/v2/updates/price/latest` and the v1 `api/latest_price_feeds` both return
**401 unauthorized**; only the feed *listing* (`/v2/price_feeds?query=`) answers. The feed ids exist, including
`Crypto.AAPLX/AAPL.RR` (the token-vs-share ratio), `Crypto.AAPLX/USD`, `Crypto.AAPLON/USD`,
`Equity.US.AAPL/USD`. So a Pyth-priced build needs a key, and the key is the bounty prize: chicken and egg.
Jupiter's price v3 returns both sides free and needs no account.
